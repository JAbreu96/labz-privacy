# LABZ (Cypher Companion) — How-To Guide

**LABZ** is a practice tool for street-style dancers. Set a tempo, and LABZ calls out
movement **directives** — Arms, Rock, Half, Bounce — in time with the beat, so you can focus
on your body instead of your brain. On top of that it records your practice, analyzes your
songs, tracks your streaks and milestones, and keeps a reference of classic moves.

A single practice session is called a **lab**.

## Quick Start

1. From the **Main** tab, tap **Start Lab**.
2. Play music and tap the large circle in time to the beat — at least 4 taps — until the BPM
   locks in. (Or let LABZ detect it; see below.)
3. Tap **Set Downbeat** on beat 1 of the next bar.
4. Tap **Start Session** to begin. After a short count-in, directives appear and are spoken in
   time with the beat.
5. Tap **Finish Lab** when you're done, then tag how it felt on the recap.

## Starting a Lab: Setting Your BPM

Tap **Start Lab** to open the tempo screen. LABZ needs a BPM before it can call directives in
time — set it any of three ways.

![Tap BPM screen with the large TAP circle and BPM readout](/labz-privacy/images/start-lab.png)
*Setting the tempo*

### Tap BPM

The large circle is your tap target.

- **Tap in time** to the music. LABZ uses the interval between taps to calculate the BPM.
- It needs at least **4 taps** to stabilize. On the Hip Hop profile, tap the **snare (beats 2
  and 4)**.
- Stop tapping for **3 seconds** and the buffer resets so you can start fresh.
- Taps outside the **60–200 BPM** range are filtered out.

### Detect from Audio

Tap **Detect from audio** to let the microphone do the work. LABZ listens to the music in real
time and updates the BPM as it goes. Pick the genre profile that matches what you're dancing to
— **Hip Hop, Tresillo, House, or UK Garage** — each tunes the detector's range and feel. Works
best where the music is the dominant sound.

### Detect from Track

Tap **Detect from track** to read the tempo of whatever is **currently playing** — start a song
in Apple Music first, then tap it. LABZ reads that track's tempo without you tapping a thing.

### Adjusting BPM

Once a BPM is set you can refine it:

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| **BPM ×2** | Doubles the BPM (e.g. 70 → 140) |
| **BPM ÷2** | Halves the BPM (e.g. 140 → 70) |
| Tap the BPM number | Type any value directly |

BPM is clamped to **40–300** regardless of method.

> **Tip:** If the detected BPM feels like half or double what you expect, use ×2 / ÷2 — common
> with tracks that have a strong half-time or double-time feel.

### Setting the Downbeat

The downbeat is **beat 1** of the bar. Setting it tells LABZ where the phrase starts so
directives fire on musical boundaries, not mid-bar.

1. Find beat 1 of the next bar.
2. Tap **Set Downbeat** on that beat — the screen flashes white to confirm, and a short 8-beat
   count plays back so you can hear that your timing is locked to the music.
3. Nudge it with **−10 ms / +10 ms** if it's slightly off.

You can re-set the downbeat anytime, including **mid-lab**, from the top control bar.

## Directive Sets

Directives are the movements LABZ calls out. They're organized into **sets**. Three presets are
built in and can't be edited or deleted:

| Set | Directives |
|---|---|
| **Isolations** | Arms, Shoulders, Chest, Head, Hips, Knees |
| **Tempo** | Eighth, Quarter, Half, Full |
| **Hip Hop Fundamentals** | Bounce, Rock, Roll, Levels |

![Directive Sets screen with presets and A/B bank badges](/labz-privacy/images/directive-sets.png)
*Directive Sets*

**Create your own:** tap **New Set**, name it, then type a directive and tap **+** to add it.
Names can be anything — you're not limited to the presets. Tap a custom set to rename it, add,
or remove directives; swipe left to delete it.

Assign any set to **Bank A** or **Bank B** with the A/B badge (see the next section). Within the
Sequencer you can play a set's directives **in order (Sequenced)** or **shuffled (Random)** so
every lab feels different.

## Setting Up a Session

Tap **Session Info** (from the Main tab, or the sliders icon during BPM setup) to configure what
LABZ calls out.

![Session Info: banks, Change Every, Cue Order, camera](/labz-privacy/images/session-setup.png)
*Session setup*

- **Banks A & B** — Bank A is the primary pool and is required. Bank B is optional; when active,
  LABZ announces **two directives at once** (e.g. *"Shoulders plus Half"*). A set belongs to A
  or B, not both.
- **Change Every** — how often a new directive is called: **8, 16, 32, or 64 beats**.
- **Cue Order** — the relationship between the spoken directive and the apply-cue beep:
  *Announce then Cue* (spoken early as a heads-up, beep on the switch), *Directive Only* (spoken
  on the switch, no beep), *Cue then Announce* (beep first, spoken after), or *Cue Only* (just
  the beep — pure improv).
- **Camera** — Front or Back, for recording.
- **Directives in video** — burn the active directive text into the recording. You can also drag
  to reposition it.

## Running a Lab

With the tempo set and the session configured, tap **Start Session**. The screen goes
full-screen for the directive display.

![Full-screen directive during a lab](/labz-privacy/images/in-session.png)
*Training in a lab*

**Visual cues**

| Flash | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **White flash** | Downbeat — beat 1 of every bar |
| **Green flash** | Apply cue — switch to the new directive now |

The current directive shows in large text; with Bank B active, the second directive appears
alongside it. Before the first call, the screen reads **GET READY**.

**Audio cues** — text-to-speech announces the directive; a short beep marks the apply cue
(silent in *Directive Only* mode).

**Mid-lab controls** (top bar): **Finish Lab** to end and see your recap · the **record dot** to
start/stop filming · **Set Downbeat** to re-sync · the **sliders** to change banks or interval ·
the **camera** to switch lens. If the music changes while you train, LABZ offers a non-blocking
banner to re-time to the new track.

## Recording Your Practice

Film a lab so you can review your movement afterward.

1. During a lab, tap the **record dot** in the top bar.
2. The first time, grant **camera** access when asked (if you declined before, you'll be sent to
   Settings). A live preview appears behind the directive text.
3. Tap the dot again to stop. The clip saves to your **Photos** library.
4. With **Directives in video** on, the active directive text and a LABZ watermark are **burned
   into the clip**, so you can see what was called while reviewing.

You can start and stop recording several times within one lab.

## Finishing and Reflecting

Tap **Finish Lab** and the recap appears: your **duration**, the **BPMs** you trained at, how
many **directives** fired, and your **most-trained** moves. Labs of **15 active minutes or more**
earn a **credit** toward your weekly goal and keep your streak alive.

![Lab recap with stats, credit, and mood tags](/labz-privacy/images/recap.png)
*The recap*

Tag how it felt — **On Fire, In the Pocket, Grinding,** or **Off Beat** — and jot a note. Recaps
and notes are saved to your **Journal** so you can look back on what you worked on.

## Song Analysis

Import your own tracks to drill against a locked beat grid.

![Song Analysis: waveform, tempo, downbeat, and cue points](/labz-privacy/images/song-analysis.png)
*Analyzing a song*

1. Tap **Song Analysis → Import Audio** and pick a file. LABZ analyzes its tempo and downbeat,
   and draws a **waveform**.
2. **Fix the tempo** if needed — **÷2, ×2, ±1**, type a value, or **Re-analyze**.
3. **Set the downbeat** at the playhead and nudge it **±10 ms** so the beat grid lines up.
4. Drop named **cue points** (Intro, Verse, Drop…) at the parts you want to drill.
5. Tap **Use in Session** to start a lab that plays the track aligned to its grid — and begins
   directives right from the cue you chose.

## Goals

Set a direction for your practice from the **Personal** tab.

![Goals: today's focus, weekly focus by day, long-term goals, reminders](/labz-privacy/images/goals.png)
*Goals*

- **Today's Focus** — a one-line intention for the day.
- **Weekly Focus** — a recurring focus per weekday; it fills in Today's Focus automatically.
- **Long-Term Goals** — bigger goals with progress bars and a target date.
- **Reminders** — practice reminders, streak-at-risk alerts, and a midweek nudge. LABZ won't
  nag: at most one a day, and nothing on days you've already practiced.

## Progress and Milestones

Track the work over time.

![Progress: lifetime totals, streak, weekly goal, milestones](/labz-privacy/images/progress.png)
*Progress*

- **All Time** — total labs, lifetime practice time, and your current day streak.
- **This Week** — labs toward your weekly goal (default 4), with the goal adjustable.
- **Milestones** — a 21-item catalog you unlock as you put in the hours (First Lab, Deep
  Session, No Days Off, Century Club, Veteran…). Tap **See all** for the full set.

Your daily streak has a **rest token** that can bridge a single missed day.

## Tips and Best Practices

**BPM accuracy**
- Tap on the kick or snare for consistent taps; avoid the hi-hat when the tempo feels ambiguous.
- Use ×2 / ÷2 to correct a reading that's exactly half or double the real tempo.

**Downbeat timing**
- The downbeat is the most important setup step for musical feel — listen for a bar before you
  tap it.
- Drifting during a long lab? Re-tap **Set Downbeat** without stopping; it re-syncs on the fly.

**Change Every**
- **16 beats** is intense — good for reaction time. **32 beats** is the natural default for most
  hip hop. **64 beats** suits slow jams or committing fully to each call.

**Banks**
- Layer a challenge: Isolations in Bank A, Tempo in Bank B, and LABZ calls things like
  *"Shoulders plus Half."* Keep Bank B empty for a simpler, single-directive lab.

**Get the credit**
- A lab counts toward your streak and weekly goal at **15 active minutes** — keep the screen up
  and keep moving.
