## Frequently asked

**The detected tempo is wrong.**
Room noise, reverb, or a heavy sub can throw off mic detection. Try tapping the tempo by hand, or let LABZ read it from Apple Music for a cleaner reading. Imported songs get a fuller analysis you can also correct manually.

**The mic isn't picking up music.**
Microphone access is optional and has to be granted. Check **iOS Settings → LABZ → Microphone**. You can always tap the tempo instead.

**How does LABZ get the tempo from my music?**
Apple Music works on-device with no setup once media access is granted — LABZ reads only the *currently playing* track. You can also tap the tempo, detect it through the mic, or import a song for a full analysis.

**Where do my recordings go?**
To your own Photos library. LABZ never uploads them.

**How do I drill a specific part of a song?**
Import the song in Song Analysis and set named cue points on it. You can start a session from any cue, so the directives begin right where you want to work.

**How do I back up or move my data?**
Practice history, journals, goals, songs and cue points live on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it — there's no account or cloud copy, so export anything you want to keep first.

**Privacy — what leaves my phone?**
Almost nothing. Full detail is in the [Privacy Policy](https://jabreu96.github.io/labz-privacy/).

**I want a diagnostic report or feedback I sent deleted.**
Email me with the approximate date and your device model and I'll remove it.
